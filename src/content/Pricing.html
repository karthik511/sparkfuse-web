Questions about money.

Why a range and not a price?
Because the fitted 30% depends on your floor. After discovery you get one fixed number per phase; it doesn't move unless the scope does, in writing.
What does discovery cost?
The first conversation is free. A full on-site discovery (one to two days, fourteen interviews, a written map) is priced on its own and credited against the first phase if you go ahead.
How do you bill a project?
Per phase: part on start, the balance when the phase is live and your team is running it. Not on hours.
Can a retainer be paused?
Yes, with a month's notice. Monitoring of what's already live continues at a reduced rate so nothing you've built goes unwatched.
Do you charge for the modules you reuse?
No. The ready 70% is why the fee is what it is. You pay for fitting, rollout and the outcome, not for re-building what already exists.
Who is on the team?
The founder scopes, designs and leads every engagement; builders work under him. You never get handed to an account manager.
Tell us the process that hurts.

One conversation, then a scoped proposal with a fixed number. Nothing locked in.
