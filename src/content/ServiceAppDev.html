What an MVP engagement looks like

Twelve weeks to something on the store, with the economics worked out first.

Weeks 1–2
Concept and economics

The 10–14 screens that matter, the unit economics per user (especially where AI is per-minute or per-token), and what we deliberately leave out.

Weeks 3–8
Build

Native mobile (both stores from one codebase), auth with phone OTP and Google, payments, the backend, and the AI piece — fine-tuned where it needs to be.

Weeks 9–11
Closed beta

Fifty to two hundred real users; retention, cost per user and the crash log fixed before anyone pays.

Week 12
Store and growth loop

Store listing, analytics, the share card or referral loop, and a month of monitoring against the number we agreed.

Questions founders ask.

Do we need an AI feature?
Only if it earns its place. Half the concepts we see are better without one in v1; we'll say so, and design where it goes in v2.
What will the AI cost per user?
That's the first thing we model — per-minute voice, per-token text, per-image vision — against your price point. If it doesn't work at ₹299 a month, you'll know in week one.
Can you build both Android and iOS?
Yes, from one codebase, with native performance where it matters (video, audio).
Do you take equity?
No. Project-based, phase-cut, you own the code.
Can you build an enterprise app for our floor?
Yes — inspection apps, dealer portals and field apps are the same craft. See the product pages.
What happens after launch?
A month of monitoring is included; a retainer follows if you want continuous build. Or your own team takes over the codebase — we hand it over properly.
