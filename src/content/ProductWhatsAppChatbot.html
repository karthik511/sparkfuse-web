Questions support heads ask.

Will it make things up?
It answers only from lookups in your systems; if the answer isn't there, it says so and hands over. It never improvises a price, a date or a policy.
What if a customer is angry?
Anger, complaints and "call me" are handoff triggers by default. A person gets the thread and an SLA; the customer gets a name.
Can it take orders or bookings?
Yes, for structured ones — a dealer re-order, a table booking, an appointment — written into your system and confirmed back.
Does it work on our website too?
Same brain, a chat widget on the site — with the same rules and the same handoff.
What does the API cost?
Per conversation, billed by the platform at cost; service conversations are cheap. Shown before go-live.
Is our data used to train anything?
No. Lookups run against your systems in your account; nothing is shared across clients.
