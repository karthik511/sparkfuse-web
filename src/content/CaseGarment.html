The structural change

The old process depended on people remembering to update things. The new one updates itself and asks for a human only when a decision is genuinely needed — which is the difference between software that gets used and software that gets abandoned.

What the AI actually does
Orchestrates the plan. Builds the time & action schedule per order and re-plans when dates slip.
Creates and assigns the work. Tasks appear for the right person at the right stage, without a manager writing them.
Reads the inputs. Pulls status out of email and buyer portals so the system updates itself.
Flags what matters today. Surfaces the handful of things that need a human decision.
