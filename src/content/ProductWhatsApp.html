Built for a garment exporter in Tiruppur. Here's what it does.

Orders, sampling updates and buyer changes arrived on WhatsApp and email and were re-keyed into one spreadsheet per order. Now the system reads them, re-plans every affected stage, creates the work for the right person and drafts the chase — and asks for a human only when a real decision is needed.

Questions buyers ask.

Does it work on our existing WhatsApp number?
Yes, through the WhatsApp Business (Cloud) API. Your team keeps using WhatsApp; the system reads and writes alongside them.
What if the AI misreads a message?
Anything that changes a date, quantity or price starts in "ask first" mode — it drafts, a person approves. You decide, per action, when it may act alone.
Hinglish, Tamil, buyer shorthand?
Yes. It's tuned on your own threads during the build — your codes, your abbreviations, your buyers' phrasing.
Does it replace our ERP or sheet?
It writes into whatever you run — a sheet, Tally, an existing ERP, or one we build. It replaces the re-typing, not your system.
Is our data used to train anything?
No. Your threads stay in your own account; nothing is shared across clients.
How is this priced?
Project-based, cut into phases, each a finished piece — or a monthly retainer if you want continuous build and monitoring. See Pricing.
