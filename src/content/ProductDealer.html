Questions sales heads ask.

Our dealers won't use an app.
They don't have to. The portal runs in a phone browser from a WhatsApp link, and an order sent as a WhatsApp message or a photo of a slip is read into the system the same way.
What happens to the sales reps?
They stop taking dictation and start selling: the system tells them each morning which dealers are up, down, quiet or overdue in their territory.
Can it handle schemes and claims?
Yes — schemes apply at order time by your rules, and claims settle against invoices with an audit trail.
Multiple depots and transporters?
Standard. Allocation picks the nearest stock by your rules; dispatch works with whichever transporters you use.
Does it replace Tally?
No. Invoices and receipts post to Tally; this runs the order and dealer side.
Can we start with one territory?
That's how we always start — one territory, thirty dealers, then the network state by state.
