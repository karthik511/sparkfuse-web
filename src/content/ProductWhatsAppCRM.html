Questions sales teams ask.

Can reps keep their own numbers?
The business number is the one customers write to and the one the CRM reads. Reps can still call from their phones; the chat stays on the shared inbox where it can be assigned and seen.
What's an approved template?
The first message to a customer outside a 24-hour window must use a template approved on the WhatsApp platform. We write and register them for you; free-form replies are allowed once the customer has written in.
Does it send bulk marketing?
No — it runs conversations with people who wrote to you or opted in. Broadcast tools get numbers banned; this is built to keep yours.
Can it work with the CRM we have?
Yes — it writes leads and activities into it. If you don't have one, the built-in pipeline is enough for most teams.
What does the API cost?
Per conversation, billed by the platform at cost — typically a few hundred to a few thousand rupees a month for a sales team. Shown before go-live.
Hindi, Gujarati, Tamil?
Yes — intent reading and templates in the languages your customers write in.
