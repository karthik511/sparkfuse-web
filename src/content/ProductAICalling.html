Questions buyers ask.

Does it sound like a robot?
No. It's a natural voice, it pauses, it handles interruptions, and it says it's an AI assistant when asked. Callers stay on because the conversation is useful, not because they're fooled.
Is outbound calling allowed in India?
Yes, to people who have enquired or consented, with the caller-ID and content rules followed. We set it up compliant — registered templates, opt-outs honoured, DND respected — and won't run cold-calling campaigns to bought lists.
What happens when it doesn't understand?
It asks once, then hands over — to a person on the line or a call-back with the transcript attached. The handoff rate is a number we track and drive down weekly.
Which CRM does it write to?
Yours — a sheet, Zoho, a real-estate CRM, or one we build. The record shape (temperature, next action, summary) is fitted to your pipeline.
What does a minute cost to run?
Telephony plus the voice pipeline, roughly ₹1–4 a minute depending on volume and language, billed at cost and visible before go-live. We publish how we cut it in the Lab.
Can we hear it before deciding?
Yes — on the demo call we'll run it against your own script, live.
