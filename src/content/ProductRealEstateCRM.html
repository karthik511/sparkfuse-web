Questions developers ask.

Will buyers mind being called by an AI?
They mind waiting until tomorrow more. The agent says it's an assistant, calls within seconds, books the visit, and hands the buyer to a named person. Visit rates go up because nobody is forgotten.
We work with brokers. Does it handle them?
Yes — broker leads are tagged at source, commissions tracked against bookings, and brokers get their own status updates on WhatsApp.
Which portals does it pull from?
Any that email or export leads, plus your website forms and missed-call numbers. New sources are added in an hour.
Can the team keep using their sheet?
For the first weeks it stays in sync as a backup. By week five nobody opens it.
Does it manage inventory and tokens?
Yes — units, holds, tokens and bookings, so sales and accounts see the same availability.
What does calling cost to run?
Telephony and voice minutes at cost, roughly ₹1–4 a minute, shown before go-live.
