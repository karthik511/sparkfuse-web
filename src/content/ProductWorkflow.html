Questions owners ask.

Will my team feel policed?
The chases go to suppliers and buyers first; internally it creates tasks and asks questions, it doesn't grade people. Teams tend to like it because it takes the remembering off them.
What if it chases the wrong person?
Every action starts in "ask first" mode and is promoted one at a time once you've seen it behave. Ownership rules are yours to edit.
Does it need an ERP?
No. It reads from what you have — sheets, email, WhatsApp, Tally — and writes tasks and digests. An ERP makes it stronger, not necessary.
How does it know a supplier is late?
From evidence: no goods-receipt against a due date, no reply to a message, a portal status unchanged — not from someone remembering to tick a box.
Can it make phone calls?
Yes — escalation can place a call through our AI calling product, with the transcript logged against the order.
Who sees the audit log?
You. Every message, task and decision the system took is logged with the evidence it acted on.
